## The finding (P1 noindex — diagnosed from the dump)

`products` on the 14/09 dump: 11,368 rows, all `publier=1`.

| seo_robots_index | rows | in stock | body ≥250 words | who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4,597 | 20 | 4,315 | iHerb import, indexed by the measured gate |
| NULL (→index) | 205 | 56 | 133 | legacy hand-built, never edited since April |
| 0 | 6,566 | **69** | 76 | 6,471 iHerb stubs (correct) + **95 legacy hand-built (a bug)** |

Only ~145 products in the whole catalogue are in stock, and **69 of them are noindex** — every
one a legacy hand-built product. They are the store's actual sellers: Serious Mass 5.45 kg (97
order lines), Gold Standard Whey 2.27 kg (64), Levro Legendary Mass 6.8 kg (63), Mass Gainer Zero
7 kg (45), Micronised Creatine ON (43), Creatine Quamtrax 500 g (39)… Verified live with a
Googlebot UA: `noindex, follow`, absent from the sitemap; meanwhile Serious Mass 2.7 kg
(out of stock, BackOrder) is `index, follow`.

**Root cause — a Filament form bug, not an admin decision.** `seo_robots_index`/`_follow` are
nullable, NULL on every pre-April-2026 product. The API reads NULL as "index, follow"
(`Product::getEffectiveSeoRobotsIndexAttribute`), but the Filament Toggle hydrates NULL as OFF
and saves OFF as `0`. So any edit of a legacy product (price, stock) silently wrote
`noindex, nofollow`. `seo:products-robots-audit --apply` then repaired follow → 1 and, by its own
design, left index at 0. Evidence: all 95 are at exactly (0, 1); 0 of the 205 never-edited legacy
products are at 0; all 95 have `updated_at` in the last four weeks; the count grew from 49
(measured 10/08 in the repo's own docblock, attributed to "human decisions") to 95 today.

All 95 bodies are unique (95 distinct hashes); 81 clear the repo's 250-word gate, 14 don't.
