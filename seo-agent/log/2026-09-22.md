## What I changed, and why

**A. The URL-case contract — kills the loop at builder level (finding 1).** Salvaged from
`origin/seo/daily-2026-09-21` **by file, not by merge**, and audited hunk by hunk: that branch
predates `main`, and taking its `productUrl.ts` wholesale would have **deleted the `'affiliate'`
reserved-route entry** `main` added afterwards. Re-inserted with its comment; the net diff now
adds and changes only.

- `frontend/src/util/productUrl.ts` — new `urlSlug()` (trim + lowercase) and `sameUrlSlug()`;
  `getProductLink`, `buildProductUrl`, `buildProductUrlPath` and both breadcrumb URLs fold the
  taxonomy segment; `isProductInSubCategory` compares through `sameUrlSlug`. Product slugs are
  **not** folded (none carries a capital; folding one that did would trade a loop for a 404).
- `frontend/src/app/(shop)/[slug]/[productSlug]/page.tsx` — the two
  `subCategory.slug !== cleanSubCatSlug` guards (metadata + page) now use `sameUrlSlug`. These
  were the 308 that closed the loop.
- `frontend/src/app/x-crawler/product/[...slug]/page.tsx` — same guard, so Googlebot's view and
  the browser's agree (the playbook's both-views rule).
- `frontend/src/util/sitemapSources.ts` — product entries emit the folded segment, so Google is
  handed `/intra-workout/…` directly instead of a URL that 301s.
- `frontend/src/app/(shop)/category/[slug]/page.tsx` — the `bestProductSlugs` href is folded
  (`shopJsonLd.ts` already routes its `ItemList` urls through `getProductLink`, so that surface
  is covered by the util change).
- `frontend/src/util/productComparison.ts` — the comparison table's `categoryUrl` is folded, so
  that template stops emitting a 301 source.
