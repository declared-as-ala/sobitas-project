  `unstable_cache` expires. One-line fix: let `wasChanged('publier')` through the gate. Not done
  today (filament/, untestable locally beyond lint).

  stock legacy products across the catalogue = candidates for the same retire-or-restock decision.

  Tunisie`). Both link `/omega-3` (good). Consolidate the thinner into the richer (P3).
